Install CaptureAsk v0.3.6

    Download v0.3.6 for Chrome

    Capture the right level of context.

    Use Full screen & send for your monitor, Current tab for a preview, or Drag to capture & send for an exact part of the current page.

    Why does Chrome show Share?

    Chrome requires you to select the monitor and click Share before an extension can capture the full screen. This confirmation cannot be bypassed. After you click Share, CaptureAsk continues with the selected AI automatically.

    Choose your AI service

    Choose ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok before capture. CaptureAsk opens the selected service and prepares the capture for attachment. Sign-in, upload limits, and data handling are governed by that service.

    Your image. Your decision.

    The extension captures only after you ask it to. Selecting an AI opens that service and prepares the capture for attachment; AI login, upload rules, and data handling remain governed by that service.

    Content first. Advertising second.

    If the site is approved for advertising, ads appear only on substantial editorial guides and are clearly separated from download, installation, and capture actions. The extension contains no advertising.
