INSTALL & USE

Ready in three steps.

    THREE CAPTURE MODES

    Capture the right level of context.

    Use Full screen & send for your monitor, Current tab for a preview, or Drag to capture & send for an exact part of the current page.

    ChatGPTClaudeGeminiGrok

    FULL-SCREEN CAPTURE

    One required Chrome confirmation.

    To protect you, Chrome requires you to select your monitor and click Share. CaptureAsk cannot skip this system confirmation. After Share, the chosen AI opens and the capture is prepared for attachment.

    01

    Install

    Download the ZIP, extract it, then use Chrome Extensions → Developer mode → Load unpacked.

    02

    Send to AI

    Choose ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok before capture. CaptureAsk opens the selected service after capture.

    03

    In your control

    A capture is created only when you click a capture action. Review sensitive information before sharing it with an AI service.

    04

    Need help?

    Read the setup and privacy guide before using the extension on sensitive pages.
