CAPTUREASK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

CaptureAsk is a Chrome extension and a help site for screen capture workflows. We design it so that you decide when a capture is created and when it is shared.

Extension captures and temporary storage (v0.3.7)

Screenshots are created only after a capture action. Within session-storage capacity, the last preview is stored in Chrome browser-session memory until replaced, discarded with “Discard & capture again”, or the browser closes. A pending AI handoff is available for two minutes and is read only by the AI tab opened for that capture. An expired handoff is removed when it is read or another capture is prepared; expiration does not delete the recovery preview. The selected AI preference is stored locally. The extension clears the legacy on-disk screenshot queue when updated, started, or opened.

For tab or screen previews exceeding session-storage capacity, the popup tells you to download before closing. A selected page area exceeding that capacity stops without opening an AI tab and shows a notice on the source page: reopen CaptureAsk and recapture a smaller area or save a tab preview. Clipboard copies and downloaded files remain outside extension storage; clear them yourself if needed.

Permissions and sharing

activeTab and scripting support visible-tab capture and the selection overlay. desktopCapture asks Chrome to let you choose a screen. downloads saves a file you request; clipboardWrite copies an image you request. storage keeps the preview and selected AI preference; tabs opens the chosen AI. offscreen crops a selected area. Host permissions and content scripts on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok attempt to assign your capture to an image-upload control only in the tab opened for that capture. No chat message is submitted automatically. Selecting “send” or a capture-and-send action may upload the image through that AI’s own page even before you submit a question. Opening a tab or assigning a file does not confirm upload completion: inspect the image thumbnail and use the saved preview if needed.

The extension creates a screenshot only after you choose a capture action. When you choose an AI service, the extension opens that service and prepares the image for the sharing flow. The AI provider’s own terms and privacy policy govern your account, uploads, and data handling. Do not capture or share confidential information unless you are authorized to do so.

Website analytics and advertising

The current checked-in site does not load analytics or advertising scripts. The ownership metadata and ads.txt file do not themselves serve ads. If enabled in the future, this site may use analytics and Google AdSense. These services can use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to measure traffic, prevent fraud, and provide or personalize advertising. Third parties, including Google, may place and read cookies in your browser as a result of ad serving.

When advertising is enabled, a consent notice will be shown where required by law. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and applicable consent controls.
