Capture your full screen safely

Use this mode when the relationship between several windows matters more than one web page.

How it works

  1. Choose Full screen & send in CaptureAsk.
  2. In Chrome’s sharing dialog, select the monitor you intend to capture.
  3. Click Share. Chrome requires this confirmation; no extension can bypass it.
  4. After the capture, CaptureAsk opens the AI service you selected and prepares the image for attachment.

Before you click Share

Close private chats, password managers, banking tabs, notifications, and documents you do not intend to include. A full-screen capture can include more than the browser tab you were looking at.

Ask a useful question

State what you want the AI to compare or explain. For example: “Identify the difference between these two dashboard totals and suggest the next check.”
