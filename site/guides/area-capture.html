Capture only the part that matters

Area capture is usually the best choice for a chart, message, error, product detail, or a small section of a page.

How it works

  1. Choose Drag to capture & send.
  2. Drag across the exact part of the current page you want to capture.
  3. Release the pointer to confirm the selection.
  4. CaptureAsk opens the selected AI service and prepares the image for attachment.

Why a smaller capture helps

It removes unrelated details, protects information outside the selection, and makes your question easier for an AI to interpret. Include labels, values, and enough surrounding context to make the image understandable.

Ask a useful question

Pair the image with one direct request: “Summarize this chart for a client,” or “Explain this error message in plain English and list the next two steps.”
