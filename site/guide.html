Install CaptureAsk v0.3.7

  1. Download the CaptureAsk v0.3.7 ZIP and extract it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode.
  3. Select Load unpacked, choose the extracted folder, and pin CaptureAsk if you wish.

Download v0.3.7 for Chrome

Capture the right level of context.

Use Full screen & send for your monitor, Current tab for a preview, or Drag to capture & send for an exact part of the current page.

Why does Chrome show Share?

Chrome requires you to select the monitor and click Share before an extension can capture the full screen. This confirmation cannot be bypassed. After you click Share, CaptureAsk continues with the selected AI automatically.

Choose your AI service

Choose ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok before capture. CaptureAsk opens the selected service and prepares the capture for attachment. Sign-in, upload limits, and data handling are governed by that service.

Your image. Your decision.

Within available storage capacity, captures stay in browser-session memory until replaced, discarded, or the browser closes. The AI attachment queue expires after two minutes and is restricted to the AI tab opened for that capture. Selecting an AI attempts to share the image with that provider. See the Privacy Policy for permissions and data flow.

Content first. Advertising second.

If the site is approved for advertising, ads appear only on substantial editorial guides and are clearly separated from download, installation, and capture actions. The extension contains no advertising.

Before you capture

Current tab captures the visible page and shows a preview. Full screen & send captures the chosen monitor after Chrome’s Share confirmation. Drag to capture & send selects a rectangle on the visible page and opens the chosen AI.

Check the attachment

A new AI tab does not prove that the image was attached. Sign in before capture, then look for an image thumbnail. If it is missing, use Current tab, save the preview and upload the file with the AI service’s attachment control.

Write a checkable question

Include chart labels, units and dates in the crop. Ask the AI to distinguish visible facts from guesses and to say when text is unreadable. Inspect its answer before acting.
