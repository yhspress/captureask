Getting started

    Ask better questions with screenshots

    Crop to the exact detail that matters. Add a short goal, context, and a clear question. Remove names, account numbers, addresses, and other private data before sharing.

    Choose your AI service

    CaptureAsk can open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok after copying your capture to the clipboard. Sign-in, upload limits, and data handling are governed by the AI service you choose.

    Your image. Your decision.

    The extension captures only after you ask it to. It does not automatically upload images. To share with an AI service, select it, focus the opened tab, and paste the image yourself.

    Content first. Advertising second.

    If the site is approved for advertising, ads appear only on substantial editorial guides and are clearly separated from navigation, downloads, installation, and capture actions. The extension contains no AdSense code or advertising.
