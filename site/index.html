IN YOUR CONTROL

Your image. Your decision.

Within available storage capacity, captures stay in browser-session memory until replaced, discarded, or the browser closes. The AI attachment queue expires after two minutes and is restricted to the AI tab opened for that capture. Selecting an AI attempts to share the image with that provider. Before you capture

Current tab captures the visible page and shows a preview. Full screen & send captures the chosen monitor after Chrome’s Share confirmation. Drag to capture & send selects a rectangle on the visible page and opens the chosen AI.

Check the attachment

A new AI tab does not prove that the image was attached. Sign in before capture, then look for an image thumbnail. If it is missing, use Current tab, save the preview and upload the file with the AI service’s attachment control.

Write a checkable question

Include chart labels, units and dates in the crop. Ask the AI to distinguish visible facts from guesses and to say when text is unreadable. Inspect its answer before acting.
